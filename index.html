      About

      Star Sky works entirely offline. No account, no tracking, no network requests. Star data: Yale Bright Star Catalogue (Hoffleit & Warren). Star names: IAU WGSN. Constellation figures: d3-celestial (Olaf Frohn). Magnetic model: NOAA WMM2025. Cities: Natural Earth.
